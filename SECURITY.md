# Security and Safety Policy

## Electrical safety
This project interfaces with household appliances that may expose hazardous voltages, currents, heat, moving parts or non-isolated low-voltage sections.

Do not interpret repository content as a substitute for appropriate electrical competence, isolation checks or appliance-specific safety information.

## Sensitive data
Do not commit Wi-Fi credentials, Home Assistant tokens, device secrets, private keys or reusable production credentials in ESPHome or build configuration.

Use placeholders or secret references instead.

## Reporting
Report suspected security or safety defects privately where disclosure could put deployed hardware or users at risk.
